Privacy Policy

DuSu — "Speak with Confidence" · Last updated 2026-10-15

DuSu is a voice-first AI English-speaking coach. This page explains what data DuSu collects, why, and how to have it deleted.

Who runs DuSu

DuSu is built under the Rana Brothers name — a two-person independent project, not a registered company. It is developed and operated by David Singh Rana, who is the person responsible for your data. For any privacy question or request, contact: support@ranabrothers.online.

What we collect

  • Account identity — your name, email address, and profile photo, from Google Sign-In. We never see or store your Google password.
  • Practice content — the text of what you say (transcribed by your browser, not by us) and DuSu's replies, so DuSu can hold a conversation and remember context across sessions (nickname, interests, progress, goals).
  • Your own AI provider keys (if you add them) — DuSu lets you bring your own free API keys (Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, GitHub Models) instead of relying on a shared quota. These are stored on our server, obfuscated, associated only with your account, and used only to make requests on your behalf.
  • Usage & progress data — streaks, XP, badges, level, session counts — used to run the app's coaching/gamification features.
  • Practice Room results (optional feature) — if you use Practice Room, DuSu stores for each attempt the topic or scenario you typed, the language you chose, how long it took, your scores, and the written feedback (which can quote up to three short sentences from your talk). Your recording and the full transcript are not kept on our servers — they stay on your phone until you delete them. For the conversation exercises (viva, group discussion, role-play) you speak and DuSu answers like in the talk modes; the conversation text is held only for the length of the exercise, used to score it, and then discarded — only the scores and the written feedback are stored.
  • My Day (optional feature) — if you use My Day, DuSu stores the tasks, notes, goals, habits and reminders you create, your timezone and reminder settings, and — only if you turn notifications on — your phone's push address (an identifier our server uses to send you a reminder). This data belongs to your account and is shown only to you. DuSu can read your plan inside your own conversations (for example to tell you what is left today) but never changes it. We never ask for or store passwords or credentials of any other service in My Day.
  • Resume Interview (optional feature) — if you use Resume Interview, DuSu stores the text of the resume and the job description you add — after removing contact and personal details (email addresses, phone numbers, links, ID numbers, the name at the top of a resume and "personal details" sections). This removal is best effort: a name elsewhere in the text, employers, and anything you say in your answers are not guaranteed to be removed. DuSu also stores the match analysis, the questions it asked, the text of each of your answers (a transcript), its feedback and your reports, until you delete them. The file you upload is not kept, and your recordings are not stored on our servers — they stay on your phone (see "How speech is handled").
We do not run ads and do not use any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. Nothing here is sold to advertisers.

Who we share data with

To generate DuSu's spoken replies, the text of your conversation is sent to whichever AI provider is active for your account — Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, or GitHub Models. This is a pass-through to generate a response; DuSu does not use your conversations to train any model. If you use Practice Room, the transcript of your talk is also sent to that AI provider so it can be scored — it is used for that one request and is not stored by DuSu — and your recording is sent to Groq — or, when Groq is not available, to Google Gemini — to be turned into text (see "How speech is handled"). If you turn on My Day notifications, a reminder is delivered through your browser vendor's push service (Google, Mozilla, Apple or Microsoft); the message is encrypted so that only your device can read it, and the push service sees only the device address and the size and timing of the message. If you use Resume Interview, the text of your resume and of the job description, and the transcript of each answer, is also sent to the AI provider you chose so DuSu can write the analysis and the feedback. On a provider's free tier that provider may keep what it receives, under its own policy; DuSu does not use it to train any model.

How speech is handled

Talk modes (Daily Talk, Face-to-Face, Interview, Learn): DuSu does not record, receive or store your voice. Speech-to-text and text-to-speech are performed by your browser's own speech features, and only the resulting text is ever sent to DuSu's servers. Note that most browsers (including Chrome on Android) carry out speech recognition using their vendor's online speech service, which means your audio may be processed by your browser vendor under their own privacy policy — that step happens between you and your browser, and DuSu has no access to it.

Practice Room (optional, separate): unlike the talk modes, Practice Room does record your voice, so here is exactly where it goes:

  • Recording starts only when you tap Start, shows a visible recording indicator, and pauses if you switch apps. It never records in the background.
  • The recording is saved on your phone (in DuSu's local browser storage) so you can listen back. It stays there until you delete it (Practice Room → report → "Delete this recording from my phone"), clear the app's data, or delete your account in the app.
  • To turn the recording into text, it is sent directly from your phone to Groq (a speech-to-text provider) using the free API key you added to DuSu. DuSu's servers do not receive or store that audio, and Groq handles it under its own privacy policy. (An account that uses a DuSu-provided key instead — for example the app owner's — has the audio pass through DuSu's server, in memory, for the length of that one request, only to forward it to Groq, or to Google Gemini if Groq is not available. It is never written to disk.)
  • The transcript and word timings are then sent to DuSu's server and to the AI provider to be scored, and are discarded afterwards; only the scores and written feedback are stored.

Resume Interview (optional, separate): your answers are recorded the same way — recording starts only when you tap, shows a visible indicator, and the recording stays on your phone so you can listen back. To turn an answer into text it is sent directly from your phone to Groq with the free key you added (for an account that uses a DuSu-provided key it passes through DuSu's server in memory for that one request, goes to Groq or Google Gemini, and is never written to disk). Unlike Practice Room, the transcript of each answer is stored in your account, so you can read it, continue on another device and see your report.

How your data is protected

All traffic between your device and DuSu's server is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/WSS). Your stored AI provider keys are obfuscated at rest so they are not stored as plain text in the database.

Account & data deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time — from inside the app (Profile → Delete my account) or without installing the app at all, via our account deletion page. This also deletes your Practice Room scores and reports, everything in My Day, and every Resume Interview resume, job description, answer and report from our servers (My Day and Resume Interview also have their own delete buttons in their Settings, and Resume Interview lets you delete one resume, job description or interview at a time and download everything it holds). Deleting your Resume Interview data removes the resumes, job descriptions, interviews and reports and DuSu's one-line notes about them; your XP, streak and vocabulary counters are shared with the other practice features, so they are removed only together with your account. Recordings and transcripts live on your own device: deleting your account in the app clears them from that phone too; on any other phone you used, delete them inside Practice Room or clear the app's data.

Children

DuSu is not directed at children and is not designed for users under 13. Practice Room and Resume Interview, which record audio, are intended for adults (18 and over) — college and university students and working professionals.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the "Last updated" date above will change. Continued use of DuSu after an update means you accept the revised policy.
