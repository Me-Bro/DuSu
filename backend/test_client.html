      How DuSu rewards you

      🏆 Speaker Rank
      Earn XP every time you talk — Starter → Speaker → Confident Speaker → Fluent Communicator → English Pro. XP alone isn't enough for the higher ranks — they also need real session milestones, not just points.
      📅 Weekly League & Missions
      Every week you're ranked against other speakers by XP — resets Monday. 3 personalized missions refresh weekly too; clear all 3 for a bonus XP boost.
      🏅 Achievements & Season Awards
      Badges unlock as you hit milestones — streaks, vocabulary, session counts. Every 6 months, 5 rare titles (like Most Improved, Consistency Champion) go to whoever leads that category.
      🎯 Confidence Check
      A quick 2-minute check-in anytime (More → Confidence Check) that scores your speaking and compares it to your last check, so you can see real growth over time.

      All scores are AI-estimated from how you speak — not an exact measurement.
