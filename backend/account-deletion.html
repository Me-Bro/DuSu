Delete your DuSu account

Works whether or not you still have the app installed.

Option 1 — inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open DuSu and sign in.
  2. Go to More → Profile.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

This deletes your account immediately — no waiting period.

Option 2 — without the app

Email david123rana@gmail.com from the address associated with your DuSu account (the Google account you signed in with) with the subject "Delete my DuSu account." We will delete your account within 7 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted: your profile, progress (XP, streak, badges), everything DuSu remembers about you, saved conversations, and any AI provider keys you added. This is permanent and cannot be undone.
