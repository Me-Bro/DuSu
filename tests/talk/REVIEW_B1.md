# Talk topics, band B1 - Hindi review sheet

For a native Hindi reader. **Please mark each line OK or write the fix.** Nothing here has been read by a native speaker yet (`topics.REVIEWED` stays `False` until it has).

What to look for: (1) is the Hindi natural spoken Hindi, the way a friend would say it? (2) is every Hindi line gender-neutral for the learner (a boy, a girl, anyone) - past-tense questions use the *ने* form ("तुमने क्या सीखा?") on purpose, and the learner's own sentences avoid "मैं ... गया/गई" and "रहा/रही हूँ" on purpose, so a few of them are looser than a word-for-word translation? (3) any word that is stiff, rude or wrong? (4) does the Hindi subtitle mean what the English says? Hindi words are in Devanagari, everyday English words stay in Latin on purpose.

B1 is the intermediate band: the openers are longer (up to 24 words) and ask for 3-5 sentences with a reason, so a Hindi subtitle may run to two or three short sentences.
