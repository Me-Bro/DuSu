# Talk topics, band A1 - Hindi review sheet

For a native Hindi reader. **Please mark each line OK or write the fix.** Nothing here has been read by a native speaker yet (`topics.REVIEWED` stays `False` until it has).

What to look for: (1) is the Hindi natural spoken Hindi, the way a friend would say it? (2) is every Hindi line gender-neutral for the learner (a boy, a girl, anyone)? (3) any word that is stiff, rude or wrong? (4) does the Hindi subtitle mean what the English says? Hindi words are in Devanagari, everyday English words stay in Latin on purpose.
